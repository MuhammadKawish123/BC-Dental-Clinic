Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Will I get a treatment plan before anything starts?

Yes. We look at your dental needs first, then explain the treatment plan and each step before we begin.

Do I need a CT scan or X-rays for an implant?

Usually, yes. Scans and X-rays show the bone and help us plan the implant position. We can take them at the clinic.

How long does implant treatment take?

It depends on your case and how your bone heals. We explain the expected timeline in your treatment plan.

What is All-on-Six?

A full arch of teeth supported by six implants. It is an option for people missing many or all teeth in one jaw.

Can I have a cleaning or wisdom tooth removal on the same trip?

Often, yes. Tell us what you need when you book and we will plan your visit around it.

What should I bring to my appointment?

A valid passport or passport card, any recent dental X-rays, and a list of your medications. Please check current border requirements before you travel.
